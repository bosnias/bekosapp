# What to record

Goal: every phrase said by 3 to 5 different native speakers (mix of ages, men and women, regions).
Each speaker records the whole list. Name files `<name>-<speaker number>.m4a`, for example `zdravo-1.m4a`, `zdravo-2.m4a`.
Normal speed, quiet room, phone held a hand away. One file per line.

## Saying hi

- `zdravo`: Zdravo! (Hello!)
- `sta-ima`: Šta ima? (What's up?)
  - `sta-ima-extra-1`: Evo, ništa. (Nothing much. (a common answer))
- `kako-si`: Kako si? (How are you?)
  - `kako-si-extra-1`: Kako ste? (How are you? (polite))
- `dobro-sam`: Dobro sam. (I'm good.)
- `hvala-lijepa`: Hvala lijepa. (Thank you very much.)
- `nema-problema`: Nema problema. (No problem.)
- `vidimo-se`: Vidimo se! (See you!)

### Saying hi: conversation

One speaker plays "them". Also record the answers so learners can hear a good one.

- `talk-hi-1-they`: Zdravo! Šta ima? (Hi! What's up?)
- `talk-hi-1-answer`: Evo, ništa.
- `talk-hi-2-they`: Kako si? (How are you?)
- `talk-hi-2-answer`: Dobro sam.
- `talk-hi-3-they`: Hvala lijepa. (Thank you very much.)
- `talk-hi-3-answer`: Nema problema.
- `talk-hi-4-they`: Vidimo se! (See you!)
- `talk-hi-4-answer`: Vidimo se!

## With family

- `dobro-jutro`: Dobro jutro. (Good morning.)
- `laku-noc`: Laku noć. (Good night.)
- `volim-te`: Volim te. (I love you.)
- `falis-mi`: Fališ mi. (I miss you.)
- `kako-je-nana`: Kako je nana? (How's grandma?)
  - `kako-je-nana-extra-1`: baka (grandma (Croatia, Serbia))
- `hajmo-jesti`: Hajmo jesti! (Let's eat!)

### With family: conversation

One speaker plays "them". Also record the answers so learners can hear a good one.

- `talk-family-1-they`: Dobro jutro! (Good morning!)
- `talk-family-1-answer`: Dobro jutro.
- `talk-family-2-they`: Nana te pozdravlja. (Grandma says hi.)
- `talk-family-2-answer`: Kako je nana?
- `talk-family-3-they`: Dobro je. Volim te. (She's good. I love you.)
- `talk-family-3-answer`: Volim te.
- `talk-family-4-they`: Laku noć! (Good night!)
- `talk-family-4-answer`: Laku noć.

## When you're stuck

- `ne-razumijem`: Ne razumijem. (I don't understand.)
- `mozes-li-ponoviti`: Možeš li ponoviti? (Can you repeat that?)
- `polahko`: Polahko. (Slowly. Take it easy.)
- `sta-to-znaci`: Šta to znači? (What does that mean?)
- `ne-znam`: Ne znam. (I don't know.)

### When you're stuck: conversation

One speaker plays "them". Also record the answers so learners can hear a good one.

- `talk-stuck-1-they`: Odakle si? (Where are you from?)
- `talk-stuck-1-answer`: Ne razumijem.
- `talk-stuck-2-they`: Odakle si ti? (Where are you from?)
- `talk-stuck-2-answer`: Možeš li ponoviti?
- `talk-stuck-3-they`: Burek ili pita? (Burek or pita?)
- `talk-stuck-3-answer`: Ne znam.

## At the table

- `prijatno`: Prijatno! (Enjoy your meal!)
- `hoces-kahvu`: Hoćeš kahvu? (Want some coffee?)
  - `hoces-kahvu-extra-1`: kafa (coffee (also common))
- `jos-malo`: Još malo. (A little more.)
- `sit-sam`: Sit sam. (I'm full.)

### At the table: conversation
